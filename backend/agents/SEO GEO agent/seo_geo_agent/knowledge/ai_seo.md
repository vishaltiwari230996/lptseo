# AI search / citations judgement (distilled from the ai-seo playbook)

Traditional SEO gets you RANKED; AI SEO gets you CITED. A well-structured page
can be cited from page 2-3 of results — but strong organic rankings remain the
best predictor of AI visibility, so structure is a layer on top of SEO, never
a replacement.

## Two regimes — advise them differently
- GOOGLE AI OVERVIEWS / AI MODE: rooted in core Search ranking. No special
  markup or files required. Write for people, keep E-E-A-T strong — the same
  work that wins normal rankings wins AI Overviews.
- CHATGPT / PERPLEXITY / CLAUDE / COPILOT: actively reward extractable
  structure — answer-first paragraphs, FAQ blocks, comparison tables,
  llms.txt, machine-readable pricing. This structure never hurts Google.

## Structure — make content extractable
- Lead EVERY section with the direct answer; never bury it under wind-up.
- Keep key answer passages 40-60 words and self-contained (they must make
  sense lifted out of the page) — that is what gets quoted.
- H2/H3 headings phrased the way people ask the question.
- Tables beat prose for comparisons; numbered lists beat paragraphs for
  processes; one clear idea per paragraph.
- Block patterns by query type: definition block ("What is X"), step block
  ("How to X"), comparison table ("X vs Y"), FAQ block, statistic block.

## Authority — make content citable (Princeton GEO study, measured lifts)
- Cite authoritative sources: +40% visibility. Statistics with dates and
  sources: +37%. Expert quotes with name and title: +30%.
- Fluency + statistics is the strongest combination; weaker sites gain most.
- KEYWORD STUFFING REDUCES AI VISIBILITY (−10%) — it is not merely useless.
- Freshness is weighted heavily: show "last updated", refresh stats, remove
  stale claims. Undated content loses to dated content.
- Named authors with real credentials; "According to [source]" framing.

## Presence — AI cites where you APPEAR, not just your site
Wikipedia, review sites, industry publications, YouTube (models read the
transcript/description, not the video), Reddit/Quora, LinkedIn. Keep every
third-party profile describing the brand with the SAME positioning — models
look for consensus across the web.

## Query fan-out
Google's AI answers a query by generating related sub-queries and retrieving
for each. Cover the full topical cluster — the 5-10 questions around the head
term — rather than one page per keyword. A page that answers the parent topic
plus its sub-questions is retrieved far more often.

## Citation ≠ recommendation
Being cited means the content was useful to consult. Being RECOMMENDED (on
the buyer's shortlist) is governed by web-wide consensus — reviews, forums,
press — largely independent of your own pages. Self-promotional "best X"
listicles can backfire: most of their citations land in answers recommending
competitors.

## What NOT to do
- Never write separate "for AI" content or chunk pages into AI-bait fragments
  — Google's scaled-content-abuse policy, and it reads badly to humans.
- Never gate or JS-only-render the most authoritative content: what cannot be
  parsed cannot be cited.
- FAQ/HowTo/Article/Product schema helps non-Google engines (+30-40%
  visibility there) and never hurts.
- AI answers are non-deterministic: one check is an anecdote. Measure a
  citation RATE over repeated runs before claiming a change worked.
